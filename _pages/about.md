## About Me

Shiyu Wang is a postgraduate student in Data Science and Engineering at East China Normal University, supervised by Prof. [Yan Wang](https://wangyanckxx.github.io/). Her work centers on building reliable multimodal systems for emotion understanding and deepfake detection.

Current research interests:

- Expression Recognition
- Affective Computing
- Embodied Intelligence
- Deepfake Detection
